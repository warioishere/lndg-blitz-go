NK Fee Mode

NK channels get their outbound fee, inbound discount and max_htlc from the NK job instead of auto-fees, the FLP floor, dynamic inbound, auto max_htlc and the HTLC boost. Every minute, per peer:

  • Below its target (local %) the fee goes up NK-StepUp %, above it goes down NK-StepDown %.
  • Target fee: below the target the fee rises to at most NK-TargetUp x the sold price (24 h, else 72 h), above it falls to at least NK-TargetDown x the sold price; at the target fee it holds. Without a sale in 72 h there is no target fee.
  • Lock: no decrease after a forward or balance-out of at least NK-LockFlowPct % of capacity, for NK-LockMin up to NK-LockMax minutes (longer for a higher fee and an emptier channel).
  • Sold price: the ppm the channel's outbound flow paid (forwards and balance-outs, 24 h, else 72 h). Above NK-AnchorPct % of it a decrease jumps there; below it the fee holds or falls by NK-BelowFlowPct % of the normal step.
  • Cap: NK-CapFactor x the sold price (24 h, 72 h, else 30 days), at least NK-CapMin; NK-CapMin without a sale in 30 days. Under NK-EmptyPct % local max_htlc goes to its minimum; the fee follows the rules below the target.
  • max_htlc is the highest step of a ladder down from the capacity (each step NK-MaxHtlcStep times smaller) that fits into the available liquidity.
  • Inbound discount (channels with dynamic inbound checked), while the channel is below its target: the rebalance budget with the price it sold at (forwards of 6 h, else 24 h, else 72 h) as the reference, min(fee, that price) less the fill margin, never more than its own fee. None at or above the target.
  • A change goes out at most every NK-MinInterval minutes; the policy is resent every NK-Republish minutes.
  • Other settings: on an NK channel the outbound and base fee, Max Cost, max_htlc, FLP, auto max HTLC and periodic emergency fee fields on the other pages are locked, and auto-fees, FLP, auto max HTLC, failed-HTLC boost and the periodic emergency fee job leave the channel alone. The live emergency trigger after a forward (Live Threshold, Live Increase %) also runs on NK channels; the NK rules continue from the raised fee. The Auto-Fees switch of the channel stays on: NK only runs on channels with auto-fees enabled. Dynamic inbound is the on/off switch of the NK inbound discount.
  • Rebalance budget of an NK channel, over the NK channels to the peer: at most min(fee, sold price of 24 h, 72 h, else 30 days) less the fill margin, from NK-BudgetMarginMin ppm (empty) to NK-BudgetMarginMax % of the price (full), instead of fee x max cost; min(fee, NK-CapMin) without a sale in 30 days. The raised fee of an empty channel never raises the budget.

With NK-Shadow on, the other channels get shadow decisions: what NK would set, logged and never applied. NK decision log
